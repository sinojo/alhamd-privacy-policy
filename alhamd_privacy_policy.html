haym tech ("we", "us", or "our") operates the الحمد (Alhamd) mobile application, an e-commerce service for browsing and ordering physical products. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information we collect

To receive and fulfill orders, we may collect your name, phone number, delivery address (including city or governorate), items ordered, quantities, prices, order status, and delivery instructions. If an email address is requested or supplied, we may also collect it. Where account registration is available, we process the account details needed to operate that feature. We also process messages you send to customer support.

To deliver push notifications, the app uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). FCM and associated Firebase services may process technical data such as Firebase installation identifiers, notification registration tokens, and related device/application information needed for message delivery. These technical identifiers are not payment card details.

2. How we use information

  • To accept, manage, and deliver purchases, including confirming orders and communicating delivery updates.
  • To respond to requests and provide customer support.
  • To send app-related push notifications such as order updates and service messages.
  • To protect and maintain the app and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Payment method

Cash on Delivery (COD) is the only payment method offered by the app. Payment is collected when products are delivered. The app does not collect or store credit-card numbers or online payment credentials for these purchases. We use customer contact information, order details, and delivery address to arrange fulfillment and delivery.

4. Third-party services and sharing

We use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications. Google processes some technical information in connection with this service. See Firebase Privacy and Security and Google Privacy Policy.

We may share information necessary to carry out a delivery with relevant delivery personnel or logistics providers, including the recipient's name, phone number, delivery address, and order details. We may disclose information where required by law. We do not sell users' personal information.

5. Data storage and security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect personal information and to limit access to people and service providers who need it for the purposes described above. Nevertheless, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain relevant transaction records, address complaints, and meet legal requirements. Retention periods can vary by type of information and applicable legal obligations. Once information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize it.

7. Your choices, access and deletion requests

You may contact us at haymtech.support@gmail.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. If the app allows you to create an account, you can also request deletion of that account and associated data using this email address. Some records may be retained where legally required or otherwise permitted. If account creation is available, an in-app account deletion option and a publicly accessible web deletion-request page must also be provided where required by Google Play policy; this email contact alone does not replace those mechanisms.

You can manage push notification permissions from your device settings.

8. Children's privacy

The app is intended as a general shopping service, not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

9. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy to reflect changes to the app, our data practices, or applicable law. The current version and its effective date will be available on this page.
